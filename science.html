Science

 

I study plants by taking a lot of pictures of them. My field is plant phenomics: measuring how crops grow and cope with their environment using sensors and image analysis, rather than a ruler and a clipboard (mostly).

 

Things I point cameras at

 

• Field trials, from drones carrying colour, multispectral, hyperspectral and thermal cameras

• Wheat and barley heads, counted by machine learning models (and occasionally by my students, to keep the models honest)

• Whole plants in 3D, with LiDAR scanners and photogrammetry

• Plants in pots that get weighed all day to see how much water they drink

 

Where

 

Research lead of the APPN-UQ node at the University of Queensland, Gatton campus. I run a team of engineers, computer scientists and agricultural scientists who turn imagery into data that breeders and researchers can actually use.
