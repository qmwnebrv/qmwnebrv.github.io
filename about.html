My name is Danny Smith. I used to be a photography student in Brisbane. These days I'm a plant scientist, which mostly means I still point cameras at things. The things just happen to be crops now, and the cameras are usually strapped to drones.

 

I did my PhD in Agricultural Science at the University of Queensland, and I now lead the UQ node of the Australian Plant Phenomics Network at Gatton. You can read about that on the science page.

 

I'm still interested in meeting new people, challenging myself creatively and trying new and exciting things. If you'd like to collaborate on photos, videos or science, please get in touch.
